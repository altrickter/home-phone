# Contributing

The most useful contribution to this project is a **field report**: you built one, something about your setup differed from the guide, and you wrote down what you learned. Different ATA, different provider, different country, a rotary phone that wouldn't ring, a router that needed a setting the guide doesn't mention — that's the good stuff.

**You don't need to write a pull request.** [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) with the Field Report template and put what you learned there. The issues list doubles as a knowledge base: assistants and humans troubleshooting their own builds can search it. Periodically, patterns that hold up get folded into GUIDE.md and HARDWARE.md.

A useful field report says: what hardware and provider you used, what country you're in, what worked exactly as the guide said, what differed (with the actual setting names and values), and what tripped you up. Leave out your phone number, account numbers, addresses, and public IPs — and if you attach screenshots or photos, strip the location metadata first.

**Corrections** — a price that changed, a menu that moved, a link that died — are also welcome as issues (Correction template) or small PRs.

Things I'll say no to, kindly: affiliate links, provider or product promotion, and rewrites of the guide's structure. The repo stays small on purpose.
